Design Unflattener

Image in. Editable Photoshop & Canva files out.

  1. Text becomes live type. Every headline, deck and caption is detected, read and rebuilt as an editable text layer with matched size, colour, weight and alignment.
  2. Images get their own layers. Photos, panels and shapes are lifted off the page, and the text is erased from what's behind it.
  3. Open anywhere. Download a layered PSD for Photoshop, and a PPTX or PDF you can import straight into Canva.

    Opening your files

    Photoshop

    Open design.psd. If Photoshop asks to update text layers, choose Update — the type is then live and editable. Missing fonts are free Google Fonts; install them for an exact match or let Photoshop substitute.

    Canva

    In Canva choose Create a design → Import file and upload design.pptx (best) or design.pdf. Every text box and image arrives as a separate, editable element.

    Illustrator, Figma, Affinity

    Use design.svg or design.pdf. Layer PNGs and a layout.json are in the ZIP bundle.
